Questions the Holy familyyour familyHolyyou might have

Is Ruppy free?

Yes. Free for everyone, with no ads and no subscriptions.

Do I need a family to use Ruppy?

No. Ruppy works as a personal budget for one person. Family sharing is optional, and you can add family members any time.

Does it need my bank login?

No. Ruppy reads the payment SMS your bank already sends you, on your phone. Nothing counts until you approve it in Review.

Which banks and UPI apps does it work with?

Any bank that sends payment SMS. With notification access on, it also catches payments from GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM, CRED and Amazon Pay.

Is my data uploaded?

Not unless you choose family sharing. Choose “Continue on this phone only” and nothing leaves your phone. With family sharing, your budget is encrypted on your phone before it is sent, so we can’t read it. SMS text is never uploaded.

When can I get it?

Ruppy is coming soon to Google Play, for Android.
