Delete your Ruppy account

Ruppy: Daily Budget, by Design Abbey Studio

You only have a Ruppy account if you signed in with Google, for example to share a budget with your family. If you chose "Continue on this phone only", nothing is stored with us, so just uninstall the app.

In the app

  1. Open Ruppy and tap the gear icon to open Settings.
  2. Under Account, tap Delete account.
  3. Type DELETE to confirm.

Without the app

Email designabbey.studio@gmail.com from the Google account you signed in with, with the subject "Delete my Ruppy account". We'll delete the account within 7 days and reply when it's done.

What gets deleted

On our servers: your account, your family membership and your shared family data. If you are the family owner, the family is deleted for everyone.

On this phone (when you delete from the app): your budget, your daily backups in Downloads › Daily Expense, the payments caught from UPI app notifications, your payment-reminder alerts and the widget's numbers.

Deletion can't be undone. We don't keep copies of family data.

Not deleted

  • Bank SMS stay in your phone's Messages app.
  • Copies you saved or shared yourself stay where you put them.
  • Your Google account isn't affected.

If you ask by email instead, we delete everything on our servers. To clear the phone too, uninstall Ruppy and delete the Downloads › Daily Expense folder.
